# Play Time

Ad-free learning games for a 1-year-old on an iPad. There are no ads, links, accounts, or tracking, and after the first load it works fully offline.

**Games:** ABC (letters), 123 (counting), Colors (pop the bubbles), Shapes, Animals (animal sounds), Piano, and Drums.

## Parent setup (about 5 minutes, once)

### 1. Add it to the Home Screen
1. Open the app's link in **Safari** on the iPad.
2. Tap the **Share** button, then **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
3. Open **Play Time** from the Home Screen. It now runs full screen with no browser bar and works without Wi-Fi.

### 2. Turn on Guided Access (this is the real lock)
Guided Access pins the iPad to one app so the Home gesture, Control Center, and notifications can't take her out of it.

1. Go to **Settings > Accessibility > Guided Access** and turn it **on**.
2. Tap **Passcode Settings > Set Guided Access Passcode** and choose a code. You can also turn on Face ID / Touch ID to end it.
3. Optional: turn on **Accessibility Shortcut** for Guided Access if it isn't already.

### 3. Start a play session
1. Open **Play Time** and tap the game she should play.
2. **Triple-click** the top button (or the Home button on older iPads).
3. Tap **Start** in the top-right corner. Optionally tap **Options** first to turn off the volume buttons or set a time limit.

To end it, triple-click the top button again and enter your passcode.

## Switching games (inside the app)
During a game, there are no buttons she can use to leave. To get back to the game menu:

1. **Press and hold the faint 🔒 in the top-left corner for 3 seconds.** A ring fills up while you hold.
2. Answer the simple sum (like 6 + 7) to return to the menu. A wrong answer or **Back to playing** returns to the game.

## Notes
- Turn the volume up. The app plays sound even when the silent switch is on (iOS 17+).
- Voices use the iPad's built-in speech. For a nicer voice, go to **Settings > Accessibility > Spoken Content > Voices > English** and download an "Enhanced" voice such as Samantha.

## Hosting
It's a static site. Publish this folder with GitHub Pages: **Settings > Pages > Deploy from a branch > `main` / root**.
